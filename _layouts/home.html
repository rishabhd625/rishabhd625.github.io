About

Who I am

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Cornell with a deep interest in systems that push physical limits — whether that's a turbopump spinning at 25,000 RPM, a micro-robot coordinating chemical signals in a disaster zone, or a set of tactile blocks that help a student learn to code for the first time. I thrive at the intersection of design, simulation, and hands-on fabrication, and I'm drawn to problems where the answer doesn't already exist in a textbook. My work spans liquid rocketry, aerospace propulsion research, and assistive technology, and the common thread is the same: build things that matter, build them right.
